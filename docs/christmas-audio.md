# Christmas button audio

The local `public/christmas/sleigh-bells-click.mp3` is a short mono edit of **Sleigh Bells**, Mixkit item 584. Source: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/sleigh-bells/ . Audio supplied by Mixkit: https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/584/584.wav . License: Mixkit Sound Effects Free License, https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree . Retrieved October 16, 2026 UTC.

The edit selects 1.45 seconds, filters low rumble, adds short attack/release fades, and compresses to 80kbps mono MP3 at 32kHz for quick phone playback. It is a real recorded bell effect, replacing the previous synthesized chord. The page plays it only for enabled buttons and button-style calls to action, including keyboard activation. No sound settings, effect controls or ambient playback are rendered. One decoded audio sample is reused, retriggered on each button click; native audio is a fallback for browsers without Web Audio.
